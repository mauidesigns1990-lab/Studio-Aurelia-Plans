The Studio Aurelia approach

Designed with restraint. Lived in with ease.

A multidisciplinary studio bringing conceptual architecture, interiors, and home care together under one intentional point of view.

Our philosophy

Luxury is clarity, comfort, and consideration.

We believe a beautiful home should make everyday life feel easier. From a floor plan’s first line to a room’s final styling, the best design balances emotion, function, and a sense of place.

Studio Aurelia Plans creates original conceptual home designs, visual packages, and support services for homeowners, design lovers, builders, and developers. Every project is approached as a complete experience—not just an isolated drawing or decorative choice.

Amber Smith, Studio Aurelia

What guides the work

Aurelia principles.

The studio’s design language is luxurious but grounded: strong silhouettes, calm palettes, warm materials, thoughtful circulation, and details that serve real life.

A

Originality

Concepts with a recognizable point of view rather than generic, interchangeable layouts.

B

Function

Rooms, storage, circulation, and daily routines considered as part of the design.

C

Presentation

Clear, polished visual documents that help clients and local professionals share a vision.

D

Integrity

Transparent conceptual-design disclaimers and respect for licensed local expertise.

Scope & responsibility

Conceptual creativity with a responsible handoff.

Studio Aurelia’s concepts are not a substitute for architectural, engineering, code, permitting, or construction services unless expressly stated in a signed project agreement.

Clients should retain qualified professionals in the project jurisdiction to verify structure, safety, energy requirements, zoning, site conditions, materials, accessibility, and all legal requirements.
